Sync between your PCs

No account needed. Ferna writes one encrypted file into a folder that OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox (or a USB stick) already keeps in step between your PCs. Only your passphrase opens it: neither Ferna nor the storage service can read it. Use the same folder and passphrase on each PC.

Profiles, workspace logins and apps

A profile is a whole separate browser (its own bookmarks, passwords, extensions and sign-ins) with its own desktop shortcut. For lighter separation, give a workspace its own logins: its tabs keep their own cookies, so two accounts of the same site can be open side by side.

Privacy and security

HTTPS-first
Upgrades http:// to https:// and warns before loading an insecure page. Local addresses are left alone.
Sites allowed over HTTP
Secure DNS (DNS over HTTPS)
“Automatic” encrypts your DNS look-ups and falls back to your network's DNS only when the provider does not answer, so sites load reliably. “Strict” never falls back; on some networks pages then fail on the first try.
Warn about look-alike addresses
Before opening a site you have never visited whose name imitates a well-known site or one of your bookmarks (paypa1.com, rnicrosoft.com, combank.lk.verify.top).
Warn before typing a password on a risky page
A note above the password field on pages that are not encrypted, or on a site you are visiting for the first time.
Block third-party cookies
Stops ad and tracking companies following you from site to site. Done by Chromium itself, so cookies set from inside embedded frames are covered too. If a site's sign-in breaks, turn this off. Takes effect after a restart.
Remove tracking tags from links
utm_…, fbclid, gclid, msclkid and similar are removed from addresses before the page loads.
Send Global Privacy Control
Tells every site you do not want your data sold or shared (Sec-GPC header and navigator.globalPrivacyControl). Legally binding in some places.
Fingerprinting protection
Canvas, audio and graphics-card readings get small per-site differences, and CPU/memory are reported in coarse buckets, so sites cannot recognise your PC without cookies.
Hide your local IP address from WebRTC
Video-call features may use only your public connection, so sites cannot read your local or VPN-hidden addresses.
Scan downloads for viruses
Each finished download is scanned with Microsoft Defender (Windows) or ClamAV (Linux: sudo apt install clamav clamav-daemon); a threat is removed and you are told.
Pause extensions whose update asks for more access
The extension stays off until you approve the new access.
Enhanced security mode
Runs JavaScript without the optimising compiler (JIT), which removes the code most browser attacks target. Pages run slower and WebAssembly apps (some games, editors) will not work. Takes effect after a restart.
Safe browsing: warn before phishing and malware sites
Checked on this PC against lists updated twice a day (PhishTank, OpenPhish, abuse.ch URLhaus). No address is sent anywhere. Downloads from listed sites are blocked.
Block trackers, ads and malware domains
Hide ad spaces and banners
Cosmetic filtering: the element-hiding rules of EasyList and uBlock Origin, inserted by the browser into each page.
Block YouTube ads
Removes the ad schedule from YouTube's player data before the video starts, hides ad slots, and skips any ad that still plays. YouTube changes often; filter-list updates follow.
Block pop-ups a page opens by itself
Windows a page opens more than 5 seconds after your last click or key press are blocked (Chrome's rule); the shield button lists them so you can open one.
Sites with blocking turned off

My filters

Your own blocking and hiding rules, in uBlock Origin / Adblock Plus syntax. They apply at once, and your exceptions (@@) win over the lists. Rules made with Hide this element… (right-click on a page, or the shield button) are added here.

Extra filter lists

Subscribe to more lists by address (https only), for example a regional list such as EasyList for your language. They are added to the built-in lists and updated with them.
