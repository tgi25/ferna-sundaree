Common scams in Sri Lanka

  • “Your parcel is held, pay the customs fee.” Couriers and Sri Lanka Post do not ask for fees through SMS links.
  • “You won a prize / lucky draw.” If you did not enter, you did not win. Never pay a fee to receive money.
  • “Share your OTP / PIN.” No bank, telecom or government office will ask for these. Not even on the phone.
  • “Your account will be blocked.” Open your bank's app or type its address yourself; do not use the link.
  • “Earn Rs. 10,000 a day from home.” Task-based jobs and “guaranteed returns” are the most common online fraud.

Report fraud to the CBSL Financial Consumer Relations Department or Sri Lanka CERT (cert.gov.lk).
